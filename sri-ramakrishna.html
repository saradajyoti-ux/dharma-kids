Sri Ramakrishna

Sri Ramakrishna was a great spiritual teacher who loved God deeply. As a child, he was known as Gadadhar. He loved divine stories, worship, music, drama, and joyful spiritual life.

This page helps children first meet Sri Ramakrishna before beginning lessons inspired by his life.

Start Lesson: Loving God Within Us

🌸 Short Biography

Sri Ramakrishna was born in a village called Kamarpukur. His childhood name was Gadadhar. From a young age, he loved hearing stories of Sri Ram, Sri Krishna, and other forms of God.

He grew up to become a great saint and teacher. He showed that God can be loved, remembered, and experienced through sincerity, devotion, prayer, and purity of heart.

✨ Key Moments for Children

Childhood Joy

Young Gadadhar loved stories of God and enjoyed acting them out with friends.

Deep Devotion

He prayed with great sincerity and became absorbed in thoughts of the Divine.

Teaching Through Love

Sri Ramakrishna helped others feel that spiritual life can be joyful and real.
