🌸 Loving God Within Us

Goal: Learn that God lives within us and that kindness, prayer, and truthfulness help us grow spiritually.

🎧 Listen First

Click to hear a simple introduction.

1. Why Are We Here?

We are here to love God, who lives within us.

We learn, grow, study, and work so that we can live well. But saints remind us that life becomes meaningful when we remember God and help others.

Question: What is one kind thing you can do today?

2. Thinking About God

The more we remember God, the deeper our love grows.

Even though we cannot see God with our eyes, we can feel God through kindness, prayer, and love.

Practice: Sit quietly for 30 seconds and think, “God is within me.”

3. Young Sri Ramakrishna

Learning about God can be joyful.

As a child, Gadadhar loved stories of Sri Ram and Sri Krishna. He acted them out with friends and made learning joyful.

Activity: Act out a short story of Sri Ram or Sri Krishna with your class.
