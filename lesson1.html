Why are we here?

We learn, grow, study, and work so that we can live well. But saints remind us that life becomes meaningful when we remember God, help others, and grow in love.

Thinking about God

The more we remember God, the deeper our love grows. We may not see God with our physical eyes, but we can feel God through kindness, prayer, and love.

Young Sri Ramakrishna

As a child, Sri Ramakrishna was known as Gadadhar. He loved stories of Sri Ram and Sri Krishna, and he acted them out with friends.
