1. Why Are We Here?

We are here to love God, who lives within us.

We learn, grow, study, and work so that we can live well. But saints remind us that life becomes meaningful when we remember God, help others, and grow in love.

What is one kind thing you can do today?

2. Thinking About God

The more we remember God, the deeper our love grows.

Even though we cannot see God with our eyes, we can feel God through kindness, prayer, and love.
