🌟 Meet the Great Souls

🌸

Sri Ramakrishna

Joy Devotion

Sri Ramakrishna loved God deeply and taught that all sincere paths can lead us toward the Divine. His childhood name was Gadadhar.

Children can learn from his joy, simplicity, prayer, and love for God.

Begin with Sri Ramakrishna
🤍

Sri Sarada Devi

Holy Mother Kindness

Sri Sarada Devi is lovingly known as Holy Mother. Her life teaches purity, patience, compassion, and universal love.

Children can learn how to be gentle, helpful, forgiving, and loving.

🦁

Swami Vivekananda

Strength Service

Swami Vivekananda taught courage, fearlessness, service, and faith in oneself. He inspired people around the world.

Children can learn confidence, discipline, truthfulness, and service.
