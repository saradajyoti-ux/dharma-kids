Today’s Lesson: Loving God Within Us

1. Why Are We Here?

We are here to love God, who lives within us.

We learn, grow, study, and work so that we can live well. But saints remind us that life becomes truly meaningful when we remember God, help others, and grow in love.

🔊 Listen

2. Thinking About God

The more we remember God, the deeper our love grows.

Our saints and scriptures advise us to meditate on the Divine every day. This can be difficult because we cannot see God with our physical eyes, but we can feel God through love, prayer, and goodness.

🔊 Listen

3. Choosing a Beloved Form of God

A chosen deity helps the mind focus with love.

Sri Ramakrishna taught that we may love all names and forms of God, while choosing one form that feels especially close to our heart. Sri Ram inspires compassion and patience. Hanumanji inspires courage, strength, and service.

🔊 Listen

4. Young Gadadhar’s Joyful Devotion

Learning about God can be joyful and creative.

As a young boy, Sri Ramakrishna was known as Gadadhar. He loved listening to stories of Sri Ram and Sri Krishna. He and his friends acted out these stories in a mango grove.

🔊 Listen

5. The Maha Shivaratri Play

Deep concentration can make worship come alive.

During Maha Shivaratri, Gadadhar was asked to play Lord Shiva. When dressed with matted hair, ashes, and beads, he became so absorbed in Lord Shiva that everyone felt they were seeing Shiva Himself.

🔊 Listen

6. Creativity as Worship

Creative work can help us express devotion.

Gadadhar painted, modeled small images of deities, made dolls, and learned brickwork. His life teaches us to be cheerful, truthful, creative, and useful.

🔊 Listen

Pronunciation Glossary

Sri Ramakrishna

Pronunciation: Shree Ra-ma-krish-na

A great saint revered by many as an incarnation of God.

Gadadhar

Pronunciation: Ga-daa-dhar

Sri Ramakrishna’s childhood name.

Maha Shivaratri

Pronunciation: Ma-haa Shi-va-raa-tree

A sacred night of worship dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Ishta Devata

Pronunciation: Ish-ta De-va-taa

A chosen form of God loved deeply by a devotee.

Activity & Reflection

🎨 Activity

Draw your chosen deity, or act out a short story from the life of Sri Ramakrishna with your class.

🧘 Reflection

Before sleeping, sit quietly for one minute and think: “God is within me. May I be kind and truthful.”

💛 Practice

Do one kind action today without expecting anything in return.
